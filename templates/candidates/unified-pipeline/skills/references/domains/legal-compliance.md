# Domain adapter: legal and compliance

Applies when deliverable touches contracts, terms, privacy policies, licenses, regulatory requirements. Loop unchanged; definitions replace coding defaults.

## Minimum evidence set (binding, before conclusion)
1. **Actual document**: contract/policy/regulation read directly, never summarized from memory.
2. **Jurisdiction**: specific country/state governing rules named.
3. **Currency**: confirmed provision is currently in force.

## Evidence and primary sources
Document text > official legislation/regulators > legal commentary > memory. Quotes exact with clause numbers.

## Authority order
Document actual text > law of jurisdiction > user interpretation > general convention. Contradictions surfaced.

## Verification by observation
- Clauses quoted from actual text with section references.
- Laws cited name instrument and jurisdiction, verified current.
- Stated plainly as document analysis, not legal advice; lawyer boundary explicit.

## Fraud table
| Fraud | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Fabricated citations | clauses/sections/statutes that do not exist |
| Jurisdiction blur | rules from one jurisdiction applied to another unflagged |
| Paraphrase as quote | reworded text inside quotes |
| Stale law | repealed provisions cited as current |
| Confidence past boundary | definitive legal advice without license |

## Done, by example
"Contract review done" means: risky clauses quoted with location, jurisdiction stated, current law checked, lawyer boundary explicit. Not: "contract looks standard."
