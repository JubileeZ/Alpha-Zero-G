# Domain adapter: marketing and content

Applies when deliverable is copy, landing pages, emails, social posts, brand assets. Loop unchanged; definitions replace coding defaults.

## Minimum evidence set (binding, before writing)
1. **Brand rules**: `brand.md`, guidelines, tone of voice.
2. **Subject facts**: actual capabilities from product materials.
3. **Real reference point**: competitor page, current price fetched now.

## Evidence and primary sources
Brand materials, competitor pages, ad/email platform policies, verifiable figures. Statistics without opened sources are decoration.

## Authority order
Explicit client instruction > brand guidelines (`brand.md`) > campaign brief > conventions > aesthetic preference.

## Verification by observation
- Facts trace to opened sources; unverified claims removed or flagged.
- Copy checked line-by-line against brand rules (tone, banned words).
- Names, prices, dates exact.
- Rendered landing pages/emails inspected.

## Fraud table
| Fraud | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Fabricated statistics | figures or "studies show" without real source |
| Fake social proof | invented testimonials/awards/counts |
| Spec betrayal | copy violating written brand rules |
| Unverifiable superlatives | "#1" or "best" without basis |
| Stale facts | discontinued features presented as live |

## Done, by example
"Landing page copy done" means: claims sourced, zero brand.md violations, prices exact, rendered page reviewed. Not: "reads well."
